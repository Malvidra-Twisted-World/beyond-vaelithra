# Niskara — Three Disciplines

> **Updated from DM decisions on 2026-11-10.** This is the canonical working note for the system previously called **Haki** for player familiarity.
>
> **Related:** [[Design Document]] · [[Campaign Memory]] · [[Residents/Jeff Le Beau]] · [[NPCs/Kaede Tsukishiro]] · [[Mission F Script]].

## Canon: Nature of Niskara

- **Niskara** is the setting-specific name for the power players currently compare to *Haki*.
- It was **already present during the Sundering War**, long before contemporary Kartaloka.
- **All living beings have the potential to train Niskara**; it is **not** an exclusive bloodline, species, divine gift, or inborn caste.
- **Training does not guarantee awakening**: not all trainees succeed in triggering/unlocking it.
- Only a small number of people in present-day Kartaloka are known to use it.
- Using Niskara **may attract long-dormant Divine and Infernal beings of the old era**. The mechanics, timing, awareness, and consequences remain **DM-controlled and unresolved**.
- A specific connection to Vaeil, the Vaelithra, or any individual deity is **not established** by this note.

## Structure: Three Disciplines, Two Stages

Each discipline has an **ordinary form** and an **Awakened form**. This is technique mastery, **not** the same as a Sin/Virtue resonance threshold or class level.

| Discipline | Haki comparison (OOC) | Ordinary technique — canon name | Awakened technique — canon name |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rasa** | Kenbunshoku / Observation | **Waskita** | **Pralihat** |
| **Karsa** | Haoshoku / King's Will | **Titah** | **Titah Lintas** |
| **Raga** | Busoshoku / Armament | **Bajra** | **Nagaraga** (*Raga–Naga*) |

> **Canon naming confirmed by DM (2026-11-10):** **Niskara** is the system name, with three disciplines and two stages. All six technique names are approved: **Waskita, Pralihat, Titah, Titah Lintas, Bajra, and Nagaraga**. The OOC Haki counterparts are retained only to help players understand the reference.

### Rasa — Sense / Perception

#### Ordinary: Waskita (Kenbun)

**DM-confirmed effect:** **Advantage on Perception checks concerning things within 30 ft** around the user.

- Do not turn this into omniscience or auto-detection through every barrier.
- Jeff has used it to sense Kaede and dodge Kaede's ambush in actual play.

#### Awakened: Pralihat (Future Sight)

**DM-confirmed effect:** Glimpse **5 seconds into the future** (Jeff: *Future Sight Level 1*).

- Future Sight is **Rasa's awakened manifestation**, not a separate fourth discipline.
- Action economy, frequency, limits, bonuses, advancement beyond level 1 and exact adjudication remain open.

### Karsa — Sovereign Will

#### Ordinary: Titah (King's Will / Haoh)

**DM-confirmed resolution and activation:**
- **Activation:** **Magic Action**.
- **Ordinary range:** **10 ft**; **any creature within 10 ft** is an eligible target. Whether the effect applies simultaneously to every eligible creature or to selected creatures remains open until DM specifies the target count.
- **Duration:** **Concentration, up to 1 minute**.
- Target makes a **Charisma Saving Throw**.
- **Failed save:** target becomes **Incapacitated** until the concentration ends (up to 1 minute).
- **Successful save:** target avoids incapacitation and gains **King's Will Resistance**, giving **Advantage on the very next saving throw it makes against King's Will**.
- **Resistance consumption:** the resistance is consumed on that next King's Will save. If that next save **also succeeds**, the target gains King's Will Resistance again; otherwise the prior resistance is gone.

**DC as originally stated by DM:** `Spell Save DC + CHA`.

**DC design note, NOT an override:** `CHA` is ambiguous (score or modifier), and adding CHA to a Charisma-based spell save DC may count the stat twice. A cleaner *suggested* formula is **8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier**. Confirm final DC before automation; preserve the DM's original wording until adopted.

- Breaking concentration ends the incapacitation immediately, even before the 1-minute maximum.
- No repeating save on failure has been specified. Target count, use/recharge limits, and any other activation prerequisites remain **TBD**; do not invent them.
- In actual-play continuity, **Jeff is still learning King's Will**; do not represent it as mastered.

#### Awakened: Titah Lintas (King's Will — Remote)

- **Activation:** Magic Action; **range 25 ft**, **single selected target**.
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute, with the same failed/successful Charisma save effects and Resistance consumption as ordinary Titah.
- Awakening allows the user to project King's Will farther and more precisely.
- Line-of-sight requirements, usage/recharge limits, and any further adjustments to Titah Lintas remain **DM TBD**.
- Do not automatically give Jeff this Awakened form.

### Raga — Force / Armament

#### Ordinary: Bajra (Busho)

**DM-confirmed offensive effects (Obsidian update, 2026-11-10):**
- **Activation: Free Action** (DM homebrew: no Action / Bonus Action required to activate).
- Using Bajra adds **+1 to the attack roll**.
- A successful Bajra-enhanced hit deals **+1d8 Force damage** in addition to its normal damage.
- The DM has not yet specified eligible attack types (unarmed, melee weapons, ranged attacks), how long the empowered state lasts, use limits, or which attacks it enhances. Do not treat the base damage as automatically converted to Force.

#### Awakened: Nagaraga (Raga–Naga)

**DM-confirmed upgraded offensive effects (Obsidian update, 2026-11-10):**
- **Activation: Free Action** (DM homebrew: no Action / Bonus Action required to activate).
- Nagaraga grants **+3 to the attack roll**.
- A successful Nagaraga-enhanced hit deals **+3d8 additional Force damage**, beyond the hit's normal damage.
- Nagaraga's initial and repeated **Strength Saving Throw DC = 8 + user's proficiency bonus + user's Strength modifier**.
- **On a successful Nagaraga-enhanced hit**, the selected target makes a **Strength Saving Throw**. On a failed save, the target gains the **Stunned** condition, representing the force of the impact.
- On a **successful initial Strength save**, the target avoids the Stunned condition; the hit and its Force damage still apply.
- **At the start of each of its turns**, a target currently Stunned by Nagaraga **repeats the Strength Saving Throw** against the same DC.
  - **Success:** Nagaraga's Stunned condition ends immediately at the start of that turn.
  - **Failure:** the target remains Stunned and cannot repeat this save until **the start of its next turn**.
- **Limit:** the Nagaraga user can trigger **at most one initial Stun saving throw per turn**. Additional hits that turn do not create additional initial stun saves.
- **No automatic expiration at the end of the attacker's next turn:** the current DM ruling uses repeated target-turn saves; no separate fixed maximum stun duration has been established.
- The **+3 attack / +3d8 Force** values are the confirmed Awakened values. Whether Bajra's bonus stacks with Nagaraga's bonus or is **replaced** by it is still a DM choice; the safer balance suggestion is **replacement, not stacking**. This recommendation is **not yet canon**.
- Eligible attacks, empowered duration, Nagaraga resource limits, and any maximum number of targets or conditions on use remain **DM TBD**. Do not imply that Jeff currently knows Nagaraga.

> **Balance note:** Repeated Stunned can be potent even with a save at the beginning of each target turn. The one-stun-attempt-per-user-turn limit is canon; any further escape clause or maximum duration would require a new DM ruling.

## Relationship to Sin / Virtue Resonance

- Niskara is a **trainable, independently awakened ancient power**.
- The planned **Sins & Virtues Resonance** is a separate campaign mechanic that changes according to choices, with DM-directed 2d8 allocations and opposing resonance reductions.
- **No automatic conversion** from Niskara skill to Sin/Virtue points or vice versa is established.
- The DM intends to start connecting **Mission F — No Way Out** aftermath to the larger Sins & Virtues Resonance lore, but the mechanism and concrete events are **not yet canonized in actual play**.
- Mission F's industrial facility remains of **unconfirmed origin**. Do not automatically declare it a Vaelithra artifact, Niskara source, or Sundering War research complex.

## Niskara Aura and Old-Era Echoes

**DM-confirmed:**
- Each practitioner may **describe and color their own Niskara aura**. The appearance is player-authored and does not automatically provide mechanical bonuses or establish a Sin/Virtue affinity.
- The activation of this old-era power **can lure or draw the attention of ancient Divine and Infernal beings that have long lain dormant**.
- **When and how** those beings react, what they know, whether they awaken, and whether the user is identifiable remain **DM-controlled**. Using Niskara does not automatically summon an enemy or cause a Divine/Infernal encounter.

**Optional DM examples, not events already played:** An old ward reacts, an ancient relic resonates, or an awakened observer recognizes the signature. Do not force every Kartaloka mystery to share this same source.

## Awakening Through Roleplay

**DM-confirmed:** Training and awakening progression are **conditional on how the player roleplays and develops the character**. There is no universal automatic unlock table, fixed XP cost, or required bloodline.

- Every living being can attempt to train Niskara, but success is not guaranteed.
- A practitioner chooses and describes the **color and visual appearance of their aura**.
- The DM decides whether a narrative/training milestone plausibly unlocks a discipline or its Awakened form.
- Resonance thresholds do **not** automatically unlock Niskara.
