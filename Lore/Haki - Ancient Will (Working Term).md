# Ancient Will — Haki (Working Term)

> **DM-confirmed on 2026-11-10.**
>
> **Related:** [[Design Document]] · [[Campaign Memory]] · [[Residents/Jeff Le Beau]] · [[NPCs/Kaede Tsukishiro]]
>
> **Naming:** **Haki** is the temporary, player-familiar label. The true in-world name for this power has **not yet been decided by the DM**. Do not canonize this document's English title as its true historical name.

## Canon Lore Foundation

- This power **already existed during the Sundering War**, long before the modern Kartaloka setting.
- In modern Kartaloka, **only a small number of people can use it**.
- Its use **may trigger events or reactions** because it is a power associated with the old era.
- **Exactly what reacts, when, and why is not yet established.** It can be a plot catalyst rather than an automatic punishment for activating an ability.
- Do not assume every Haki user has a specific divine patron, a bloodline, affiliation with the Vaelithra, or a connection to Vaeil; none has been confirmed.

## Jeff Le Beau — Confirmed Training

Jeff has been taught / granted access to three Haki manifestations, but **their current mastery differs**.

| Manifestation (working label) | Status | DM-confirmed effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Kenbunshokku** | Available / used in actual play | **Advantage on Perception within 30 feet around Jeff** |
| **Future Sight — Level 1** | Available / granted | Jeff can see **5 seconds into the future** |
| **King's Will** | **Still learning; not yet established as mastered** | When used, the target makes a **Charisma Saving Throw**, with DC specified by DM as **Spell Save DC + CHA** |

### Adjudication boundaries

**Kenbunshokku:** The advantage applies to relevant Perception checks within the 30-foot area specified by the DM. Do not automatically extend this to unrestricted omniscience, passive detection across walls, or infinite range. Jeff has already used Kenbunshokku to detect and avoid Kaede during their encounter.

**Future Sight Level 1:** The vision horizon is **5 seconds**. The DM has not yet specified usage limits, activation economy, recharge, or an automatic mechanical roll benefit; do not invent a hard reaction/initiative mechanic without a ruling.

**King's Will:** Jeff is **in training**. The DM gave the save type and formula but **not** the effect on a failed or successful save, target count, range, duration, or usage limit. Leave these unspecified. Preserve the DM's formula exactly; the meaning of the extra **CHA** term (modifier vs score) should be resolved before it is automated in a character sheet or VTT. Avoid silently replacing it with ordinary spell save DC.

## Actual Play Link — Jeff and Kaede

After Haki practice, Jeff accidentally sensed nearby **Kaede Tsukishiro**. Kaede thought Jeff belonged to Raiga, fled, then attempted an ambush while blended into shadows. Jeff avoided the attack using Kenbunshokku. They are **currently staring each other down**; the encounter remains unresolved.

This confirms that at least one application of the ancient power already had observable consequences in the story, but it does **not** prove any separate mystical awakening or outside faction detection has occurred.

## Potential Triggers (DM Ideas Only — NOT CANON)

Examples of *optional future* consequences of activating old-era power:

- An ancient ward reacts unexpectedly, without explaining whether it recognizes the user's presence or the technique.
- A relic or old-world sensor responds for a brief moment, leaving a discoverable clue.
- A rare surviving practitioner senses an echo and becomes curious.
- An observer knows just enough old history to recognize that Jeff is using something **uncommon**.
- Multiple uses in an ancient site could provide an opportunity for a revelation **if the actual-play situation earns it**.

**Never assume** these reactions always occur. Avoid making every mundane mission a Sundering War plot or mandating a single overarching conspiracy. A trigger should arise from a meaningful scene, location, witness, or choice.
