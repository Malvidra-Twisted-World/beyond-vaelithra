# Sins & Virtues Resonance — Beyond Vaelithra Integration Plan

> **DM-described direction (2026-11-10), not yet an actual-play event.**
>
> **Related:** [[Mission F Overview]] · [[Mission F Script]] · [[Design Document]] · [[Campaign Memory]] · [[Lore/Niskara - Three Disciplines|Niskara — Three Disciplines]].
>
> The intended long-form Sins/Virtues Resonance system is associated with the **Vaelithra Long Campaign**. The future use in Kartaloka must respect both the existing campaign's cosmology and its independently unfolding actual play.

## Confirmed Design Intent

- After the currently active **rescue / extraction mission** ([[Mission F Script|Mission F — No Way Out]]), DM intends to begin tying its aftermath to the larger **Sins & Virtues Resonance** storyline.
- A PC can gain resonance points. **DM determines which Sin or Virtue** receives the effect, according to situation/character actions.
- When awarding points, **roll 2d8** for the resonance amount.
- If the assigned result goes to a **Sin**, its **opposing Virtue loses half of the amount gained**. The precise rounding method and minimum score floor remain to be decided.
- Each Sin and Virtue has an individual **threshold**.
- Crossing thresholds influences the PC's **personality / disposition** and applies additional **modifiers to social rolls**.
- Specific threshold numbers, exact social modifiers, opposite pairs if altered from the standard scheme, whether the inverse Virtue→Sin reduction also applies, and the roll's cadence are **not yet confirmed**.

## First Exposure: Mission F Aftermath (Planned, Not Played)

- Mission F involves a survey team's extraction from an old facility in **The Fringe**; its true origin and contents have **not been established**.
- **Do not retroactively assert** the building is a Vaelithra relic, a Niskara academy, or a confirmed Sundering War installation.
- If the DM wants to start Resonance progression after Mission F, record the PCs' actual morally or emotionally significant choices first, then select the relevant Sin/Virtue and roll 2d8.
- The first overt narrative manifestations can occur later when a threshold is reached. No mandatory cutscene or forced personality rewrite is required.
- Always record **actual-play outcomes** before linking the larger metaphysical arc.

## Relationship to Niskara

**Niskara and Resonance are currently distinct systems.**

- [[Lore/Niskara - Three Disciplines|Niskara]] is a trainable potential found in living beings; awakening is rare in modern Kartaloka.
- Resonance tracks Sin/Virtue alignment and consequences of choices.
- **A Niskara user does not automatically gain Sin/Virtue points** just for using a technique.
- A link between these powers, if any, remains open lore. The two can co-exist in a character without sharing an origin.
