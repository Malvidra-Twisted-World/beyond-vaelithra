The system originates from the broader **Vaelithra Long Campaign** concept. For Beyond Vaelithra, the DM plans to introduce its first traces when the party approaches a protected location within the active rescue mission **Mission F: No Way Out**.

## Confirmed Mechanics

1. The DM determines **which Sin or Virtue** gains resonance, based on a character's meaningful choices / the situation.
2. When awarding resonance, roll **2d8**. Add the rolled amount to that trait's score. **Keep the 2d8 amount** rather than replacing it with a fixed gain.
3. Its paired opposite decreases by **floor(2d8 result / 2)**. This rule works **in both directions**, Sin→Virtue **and** Virtue→Sin.
4. Every **individual Sin and Virtue score** has its own thresholds; do not collapse a pair into one shared meter.
5. Thresholds color the character's social behavior/roleplay and introduce **additional modifiers to appropriate social rolls**. They do **not** take control of the player's decisions.
6. The DM has not separately defined the **initial scores**, numerical floor/ceiling, or award frequency. Do not mistake the 2d8 *award roll* for a starting score. **Starting at 0, with values floored at 0**, is a practical proposal only, pending confirmation.

### Worked example

If the DM awards **Envy** and 2d8 gives **11**:
- Envy score **+11**.
- Its paired Kindness score **−5** (half of 11, **rounded down**, subject to the final score-floor rule).

If instead **Kindness** gains 11:
- Kindness **+11**.
- Envy **−5**.

## Proposed Three Thresholds (DM Approval Pending)

The points are intended to accumulate over meaningful scenes rather than every ordinary conversation. With 2d8 averaging **9 points**, these thresholds make the first shift accessible while reserving the last for longer-term development.

| Individual trait score | Stage | Proposed social-roll modifier | Roleplay cue (never compulsory) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–19** | **Dormant** | None | Trait exists but has no measurable resonance effect |
| **20–49** | **Stirring** (Threshold I) | **+1** | Subtle resonance, a tendency begins to emerge |
| **50–79** | **Manifestation** (Threshold II) | **+2** | Trait becomes more prominent in relevant social situations |
| **80+** | **Embodiment** (Threshold III) | **+3** | Trait strongly colors the character's presence and approach |

**Suggested application:**
- At most **one resonance modifier per social check**. No stacking several Sin/Virtue modifiers.
- Use it **only when that trait meaningfully supports the current social action**, such as a fitting Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation, Performance, or Insight check.
- It is **not** a permanent bonus to every Charisma check. If the trait is irrelevant, no modifier applies.
- Avoid imposing forced alignment/personality changes or automatic mechanical penalties for the opposing trait. Opposition already matters through score reduction. Any future negative modifiers would need a separate DM decision.
- If two opposed traits both exceed thresholds, choose only the **single trait relevant to the narrated action** for that check; do not automatically add or subtract them together.

> **Important:** The **20 / 50 / 80** point values, **+1 / +2 / +3** bonuses, and application guidance are **proposed balance**, not yet an explicit DM ratification. The 2d8 allocation, two-way halving, round-down rule, pairing and social-only effect intent **are canon**.

## First Exposure: Mission F — No Way Out

**DM decision:** In the active rescue mission, when the party **approaches the area where something is being protected** (the area around the **Sealed Chamber / protected lower-level location**), there is **a large concentration of Old-Era energy**.

- This is a **DM-only sensory/lore hook** that can be discovered when the group moves close enough, **not** a predetermined ending or mandatory path.
- If the party approaches, describe a **dense, unfamiliar residue or pressure of ancient energy**, with intensity tied to proximity. Do not automatically tell them it is *Sins & Virtues Resonance*.
- The energy's presence is **not proof** that the facility was **built during the Sundering War**, created by a named deity, or contains a particular artifact/entity. **The identity of the protected something remains secret** until actual play supports a reveal.
- The DM intends this location to be a **gateway to the later Sins & Virtues Resonance plot**. When and how individual PCs begin receiving 2d8 resonance awards still follows player decisions and DM adjudication.
- If players never visit the protected area, **do not retroactively claim they did**. The campaign may retain the hook for a future discovery.
- [[Mission F Script]] has its own investigation/extraction outcome rules. These stay in place.

## Relation to Niskara

**Niskara and Resonance are distinct systems unless the DM establishes a connection later.**

- [[Lore/Niskara - Three Disciplines|Niskara]] is a trainable potential found in all living beings, but awakening is uncommon in modern Kartaloka.
- Resonance tracks the growth of Sin/Virtue aspects through significant character choices.
- Activatng Niskara does **not automatically** award Resonance points.
- Old-Era Echoes from Niskara can attract ancient Divine and Infernal beings; **this does not establish** the energy near Mission F's sealed location as Niskara.
