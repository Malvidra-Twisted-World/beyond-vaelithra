# Factions Structure

> Master reference for the political, criminal, occult, corporate, and institutional organizations operating within Beyond Vaelithra.
>
> This file describes organizational structure and relationships.
>
> Actual player actions may create new relationships, branches, conflicts, or organizations.
>
> Do not assume that every organization is currently known to the players.

---

# 1. How to Read This File

Each organization should be understood through several layers:

- **Organization Type** — Faction, Institution, Network, Syndicate, etc.
- **Public Identity** — What ordinary citizens believe the organization is.
- **Actual Function** — What the organization actually does.
- **Internal Structure** — Leadership and subordinate groups.
- **Influence** — Where and how the organization has power.
- **Alliances** — Organizations they cooperate with.
- **Conflicts** — Organizations they oppose.
- **Unknown Connections** — Potential or unconfirmed relationships.
- **Player Relationship** — Current relationship created through actual play.

Not every relationship is publicly known.

**Type:** Criminal / Maritime Power  
**Primary Area:** Wavedock  
**Public Identity:** Major local power controlling much of the harbor economy.  
**Actual Function:** Criminal organization with influence over smuggling, protection, docks, and local commerce.

### Structure

- Wave Family
  - Local leadership
  - Dock operations
  - Smuggling networks
  - Protection operations
  - Independent affiliated operators

### Current Status

The Wave Family is suspected of having connections to individuals who protected or assisted the Silent Tide.

Important:

> This does NOT establish that the entire Wave Family supports Silent Tide.

Possible explanations include:

- corrupt individuals;
- local branch involvement;
- bribery;
- coercion;
- protection racket;
- unauthorized activity;
- faction-level involvement.

The party discovered evidence suggesting a possible connection between Silent Tide and individuals associated with the Wave Family.

# The Scale Division

**Type:** Law Enforcement / Government  
**Primary Area:** City-wide  
**Public Identity:** Official enforcement body handling conventional and magical crimes.

### Structure

- Scale Division
  - Command
  - Investigation
  - Patrol
  - Special Operations
  - Magical / Occult Investigation
  - Administrative Units

### Function

The Scale Division provides the official law-enforcement structure of Kartaloka.

They are not omniscient.

Their effectiveness varies depending on:

- jurisdiction;
- evidence;
- political pressure;
- available personnel;
- nature of the incident.

### Relationship with Independent Fixers

The Scale Division may tolerate independent fixers when:

- jurisdiction is unclear;
- conventional police resources are insufficient;
- the incident is outside normal procedure;
- information is sensitive.

This does not make fixers official police.

# 3. Major Neutral Institution

# The Switchboard

**Type:** Neutral Institution / Independent Fixer Network  
**Primary Area:** Old Town  
**Headquarters:** Westa Tower

> **Important:** The Switchboard is neutral, but it is NOT insignificant.

The Switchboard functions as one of the most important neutral institutions in Kartaloka.

It provides a meeting point between otherwise disconnected groups.

It allows independent fixers, clients, factions, civilians, and organizations to interact without requiring direct political allegiance.

---

## 3.1 Neutrality

The Switchboard does not formally belong to:

- Scale Division;
- Wave Family;
- Adikarsa Group;
- Script Order;
- Beacon Circle;
- Night Remnant.

It may interact with all of them.

Neutrality does NOT mean:

> "The Switchboard has no power."

Instead:

> **The Switchboard's power comes from being trusted as a neutral intermediary.**

---

## 3.2 Why The Switchboard Matters

The Switchboard controls or facilitates:

- mission contracts;
- independent fixer work;
- information exchange;
- anonymous clients;
- escrow;
- reputation;
- introductions;
- dispute mediation;
- access to people who would otherwise be difficult to contact.

Because many organizations use or tolerate the Switchboard, attacking it or openly violating its neutrality may create consequences beyond one faction.

---

## 3.3 Internal Structure

### Wulan Kertadjaja

**Role:** Manager / Primary Authority

Wulan manages the Switchboard and maintains its neutrality.

She functions as:

- contract broker;
- mediator;
- information gatekeeper;
- fixer coordinator.

Her supernatural inability to lie for personal gain reinforces the credibility of the Switchboard.

---

### The Ledger

**Role:** Contract / Administrative Entity

The Ledger manages:

- contracts;
- records;
- escrow;
- anonymous agreements.

The Ledger communicates through writing appearing in its pages.

It is not merely a normal administrative object.

---

### Reno "Kabel" Amran

**Role:** Technical Specialist

Responsible for:

- technical infrastructure;
- Signal systems;
- communications;
- network maintenance;
- technical investigation.

Former independent hacker.

---

### Ibu Tumini

**Role:** Civilian Information Network

Owner of the coffee shop operating within / around The Switchboard.

Provides:

- rumors;
- street gossip;
- civilian perspective;
- informal information.

Her information should not automatically be treated as verified.

---

## 3.4 Political Position

The Switchboard is neutral.

However, its importance means:

- factions may attempt to influence it;
- factions may use its services;
- individuals may attempt to exploit its neutrality;
- clients may attempt to hide their identities;
- hostile groups may consider it strategically valuable.

The Switchboard should therefore be treated as:

> **Neutral Power**

rather than:

> **Minor Neutral NPC Hub**

# 3.7 Connection Strength

Use the following rough categories when designing future content:

### Acquaintance

The NPC recognizes the PC.

Example:
- Master Quill ↔ Iggy
- Sebastian ↔ Xenomisil

### Professional

The NPC interacts with the PC through work or institutional responsibilities.

Example:
- Hop Pop ↔ Iggy
- Graham Howard ↔ Joshua

### Personal

The NPC has an established personal relationship with the PC.

Example:
- Roy Ivanov ↔ Kevin Ivanov

### Mentorship

The NPC has a teacher / student relationship with the PC.

Example:
- Mordred ↔ Jeff

### Faction Contact

The PC has a direct personal contact inside a faction.

Example:
- Street ↔ Jeff
- Mordred ↔ Jeff
- Roy Ivanov ↔ Kevin

> Connection strength does not equal faction trust.

---

# 3.8 DM Rule — Personal Connection ≠ Faction Access

A PC knowing a faction member should create opportunities, not bypass the faction's structure.

For example:

**Jeff knows Street.**

This does NOT mean:

> "Jeff can now enter Night Remnants headquarters."

It means:

> "Jeff has someone he can potentially ask."

Likewise:

**Kevin is Roy Ivanov's son.**

This does NOT mean:

> "Kevin automatically has access to Script Order archives."

It means:

> "Kevin has a personal relationship with someone who may be able to help."

This distinction keeps faction hierarchy meaningful while still rewarding established character relationships.

---

# 3.9 Future Foreshadowing Rule

These NPC connections may be used as **soft entry points** into future stories.

Do not force every NPC into a mission.

A connection can remain dormant until:

- the relevant faction becomes involved;
- the PC actively seeks the NPC;
- a mission naturally intersects with their occupation;
- an existing world event affects them;
- the NPC independently needs something from the PC.

NPCs should feel like people who continue living in Kartaloka rather than quest dispensers waiting for the party.

# 6. Relationship Status Legend

Use the following labels when updating faction relationships:

**ALLY**
> Open cooperation.

**FRIENDLY**
> Positive relationship without formal alliance.

**NEUTRAL**
> No meaningful conflict or cooperation.

**TRANSACTIONAL**
> Cooperation exists because both sides benefit.

**TENSE**
> Existing friction but no open conflict.

**HOSTILE**
> Active opposition.

**SUSPECTED**
> Possible relationship without sufficient proof.

**UNKNOWN**
> Insufficient information.

**SECRET**
> Relationship exists but is hidden from players / public knowledge.

# 9. Design Rule

Not every faction needs to be involved in every story.

Kartaloka should contain:

- factions with active agendas;
- factions doing mundane business;
- factions completely uninvolved in current events;
- neutral institutions;
- criminal groups;
- temporary alliances;
- local organizations;
- individuals acting independently of their faction.

A faction should enter a mission because its interests intersect with the situation.

Never add faction involvement merely to make a mission feel important.
