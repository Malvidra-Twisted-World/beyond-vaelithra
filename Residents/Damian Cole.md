```
Damian Cole is a private investigator from Kartaloka. After failing to become a police officer like his father, he built a career solving the cases the authorities couldn’t—or wouldn’t—handle. His life changed when a client hired him to investigate a series of attacks against their family. The deeper Damian dug, the more his investigation pointed toward a mysterious organization known only as the Triarch, a name connected to several cases that had been dismissed as unrelated.

When Damian finally came close to uncovering the truth, he was ambushed by unknown assailants. Beaten and stabbed, he was left to die in an alley after one final warning: “The Triarch sends his regards.” As death claimed him, however, Damian was surrounded by the spirits of the Triarch’s victims. They stitched his wounds back together and dragged him back into the world of the living.

Now Damian walks with the dead at his side. They want revenge. He wants answers. Together, they hunt the Triarch.
```
