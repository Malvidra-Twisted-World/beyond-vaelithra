# Back Story
Jeff Le Beau lahir dan besar di Kartaloka, kota yang ia kenal seperti telapak tangannya sendiri. Karismatik, percaya diri, dan selalu siap dengan senyum menggoda, Jeff tumbuh di antara penjudi, traveler, pelaut, dan hustler. Sejak kecil ia belajar bahwa cerita bagus dan bluff meyakinkan bisa membawamu jauh. Hampir setiap malam, ia berada di Old Town Lounge, bermain kartu dengan taruhan kecil sambil mendengarkan cerita para traveler. Jeff jarang bermain demi uang; ia bermain karena menikmati permainan dan sensasi tidak pernah tahu kartu apa yang akan ia dapatkan. Selama bertahun-tahun, ia juga bepergian keluar Kartaloka, mengambil pekerjaan sebagai kurir, bodyguard, guide, dan sesekali cardsharp, membuatnya terbiasa hidup di mana pun jalan membawanya.

Perjalanan Jeff akhirnya membawanya ke dunia fixer profesional. Melalui kontak dan cerita yang ia dapatkan selama perjalanan, ia mengetahui The Switchboard, jaringan broker netral di bekas menara radio Old Town. Konsep itu cocok untuknya: tidak ada bos, seragam, atau kewajiban berpihak—hanya pekerjaan, bayaran, dan kebebasan memilih apakah risikonya sepadan. Dengan kartu lemparan, kinetic bo staff, dan kemampuan berbicara yang sering membawanya masuk atau keluar dari masalah, Jeff mengambil kontrak dari siapa pun yang mampu membayar. Saat di Kartaloka, ia tetap kembali ke Old Town Lounge setiap malam, dengan kartu di tangan dan cerita baru. Bagi Jeff, setiap pekerjaan adalah taruhan baru, setiap orang asing adalah calon koneksi, dan setiap situasi berbahaya adalah satu kartu lagi yang menunggu untuk dimainkan.

## Niskara — Ancient Will (DM-Confirmed, 2026-11-10)

> **Canon technique names and current rules:** [[Lore/Niskara - Three Disciplines|Niskara — Three Disciplines]].
>
> **Niskara** was formerly called **Haki** to help players recognize the concept. All living beings can train it, though actual awakening is uncommon in modern Kartaloka; the power existed during the Sundering War.

| Discipline | Stage | Canon technique name | Current Jeff status / DM effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rasa** (Kenbun) | Ordinary | **Waskita** | **Active:** Advantage on Perception involving things within 30 ft |
| **Rasa** (Future Sight) | Awakened | **Pralihat** | **Granted:** Future Sight Level 1, sees **5 seconds** ahead |
| **Karsa** (King's Will / Haoh) | Ordinary | **Titah** | **Learning:** target makes CHA save; failed save gives **Incapacitated** until concentration breaks; successful save gives **King's Will Resistance** (Advantage on a later King's Will save) |
| **Karsa** (King's Will Remote) | Awakened | **Titah Lintas** | **Not confirmed:** extended range, selective targeting |
| **Raga** (Busho) | Ordinary | **Bajra** | **Not learned by Jeff:** Free Action, **+1 attack roll**, **+1d8 extra Force damage** |
| **Raga** (Raga–Naga) | Awakened | **Nagaraga** | **Not learned by Jeff:** Free Action, **+3 attack roll**, **+3d8 extra Force damage**; on hit, **STR save DC 8 + PB + STR mod** or Stunned. Target re-saves at the start of each turn; **one initial stun attempt per user turn** |

**Naming:** All six Niskara technique names are **canon, approved by the DM (2026-11-10)**. A canon technique existing in the setting does **not** mean Jeff has learned it; refer to his status in the table.

**Titah / King's Will:** Magic Action; ordinary range **10 ft**, duration **Concentration up to 1 minute**; Titah Lintas is **25 ft, single target**. King's Will Resistance applies on the **next King's Will save only**, then is consumed, and can be re-earned by succeeding again. Jeff is still **learning Titah**, not Titah Lintas.

**King's Will DC:** DM's originally given wording was `Spell Save DC + CHA`. The exact interpretation is not finalized. See the lore note for the alternative suggestion `8 + proficiency bonus + CHA modifier`. Do not silently replace the DM formula.

**Actual play:** After Haki/Niskara practice, Jeff sensed [[NPCs/Kaede Tsukishiro|Kaede]] nearby, pursued him, and avoided a shadow ambush using Kenbun/Waskita. They remain face-to-face with **no resolved outcome**.
