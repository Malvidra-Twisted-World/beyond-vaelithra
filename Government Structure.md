# Government Structure

> **Purpose:** Basic structure of Kartaloka's civilian government and legal authority.
>
> The government exists as a separate power structure from the major independent organizations such as the Script Order, Scale Division, and Beacon Circle.
>
> This document intentionally leaves many positions and departments undefined until they become relevant through actual play.

---

# 1. Government of Kartaloka

Kartaloka is governed by a civilian government responsible for the city's public administration, infrastructure, civic services, and enforcement of civil law.

The government is separate from the major magical and civil organizations operating within the city.

The government does not directly control:

- The Script Order
- The Scale Division
- The Beacon Circle
- The Switchboard
- Other independent organizations

These organizations may cooperate with the government, but cooperation does not make them government departments.

# 4. Kartaloka Police

**Type:** Government Law Enforcement

The Kartaloka Police are the city's official law-enforcement body.

Their primary jurisdiction covers conventional civil and criminal law.

### Responsibilities

- criminal investigation;
- arrest;
- public safety;
- missing persons;
- kidnapping;
- murder;
- theft;
- assault;
- organized crime;
- traffic and public order;
- protection of civilians;
- enforcement of city law.

### Magical Matters

The Police are NOT the primary authority on magical law or magical phenomena.

When a case involves specialized magical knowledge, the Police may:

- request assistance from the Script Order;
- consult magical specialists;
- cooperate with the Beacon Circle;
- contract independent fixers;
- coordinate with other legally recognized organizations.

However:

> **Magical expertise does not automatically transfer legal authority away from the Police.**

A magical crime can still be a criminal case under civilian law.

---

# 5. Government ↔ Script Order

The Script Order is an independent organization.

The government recognizes its expertise in magical matters but does not directly control it.

Possible areas of cooperation:

- magical crime investigation;
- dangerous artifacts;
- magical containment;
- magical disasters;
- expert testimony;
- arcane research;
- classification of magical phenomena.

### Important Distinction

**Script Order = magical authority / expertise**

**Government = legal authority**

The Script Order may understand a magical phenomenon better than the Police.

That does not automatically give the Script Order unlimited authority over the criminal case.

---

# 6. Government ↔ Scale Division

The Scale Division is an independent civil organization.

It is NOT:

- the Police;
- a government department;
- a branch of the Script Order.

The Scale Division advocates for oversight and regulation concerning magical power and magical law.

### Core Ideological Position

A simplified representation of their position:

> **"Those who possess magical power should not be the only people deciding how magical power is regulated."**

They believe magical institutions require external accountability.

This creates an ideological conflict with the Script Order.

---

# 7. Script Order ↔ Scale Division

**Relationship:** Ideological Rivalry / Regulatory Dispute

The conflict is primarily about **who should have authority over magical law and magical regulation**.

### Script Order Position

Magic is complex and dangerous.

Those with sufficient magical knowledge should have significant authority over magical regulation.

Their argument may be summarized as:

> **"You cannot safely regulate what you do not understand."**

### Scale Division Position

Magical organizations should not be allowed to regulate themselves without meaningful external oversight.

Their argument may be summarized as:

> **"Understanding magic does not give anyone the right to govern themselves without accountability."**

### Important

Neither organization is automatically correct.

This is an ideological conflict rather than a simple good-vs-evil faction rivalry.

---

# 8. Government ↔ Scale Division

The Scale Division may cooperate with the government on:

- magical regulation;
- public safety;
- civic oversight;
- policy discussions;
- magical incidents.

However, the Scale Division remains independent.

Potential political tension may occur when the government must decide:

- whether to adopt Scale Division proposals;
- whether to recognize Script Order authority;
- how much magical regulation is necessary;
- who has jurisdiction during magical incidents.

- emergency response;
- civic programs.

The Beacon Circle does not represent the entire magical population of Kartaloka unless explicitly established.

---

# 10. Government ↔ The Switchboard

The Switchboard operates independently from the government.

It is legally recognized as a neutral institution / independent fixer network.

The government does not directly control the Switchboard.

### Neutrality

The Switchboard's neutrality is socially and politically important.

Government officials may use its services.

Police may interact with fixers operating through it.

Factions may use its contracts.

Civilians may seek assistance through it.

None of this makes the Switchboard a government institution.

### Important Principle

> **The Switchboard is neutral, not powerless.**

Its influence comes from:

- trust;
- information;
- contracts;
- reputation;
- connections;
- neutrality.

# 13. Design Principle

Kartaloka should have multiple overlapping power structures.

No single organization should control everything.

A situation may involve several authorities simultaneously.

For example:

> A magical artifact is stolen.

**Police:**
Handles the theft as a criminal case.

**Script Order:**
Identifies and assesses the magical artifact.

**Scale Division:**
Questions whether the existing magical regulations are sufficient.

**Beacon Circle:**
May become involved if the artifact affects the magical community.

**Switchboard:**
May provide independent fixers to investigate or recover it.

Each organization approaches the same incident from a different mandate.

---

# 14. Future Development Rule

Government structure should expand through story requirements.

Do not create departments merely because a realistic government would theoretically have them.

When a mission requires a government authority that does not yet exist:

1. Identify the minimum authority needed.
2. Establish it.
3. Add it to this document.
4. Connect it to existing organizations.
5. Record any resulting political or factional relationship.

This keeps Kartaloka's government flexible and prevents unnecessary bureaucracy from overwhelming the campaign.
