# 1. Campaign Identity

**Campaign:** Beyond Vaelithra  
**Setting:** Kartaloka, Dewanegara  
**Format:** West Marches-inspired Async PbP  
**Campaign Type:** Persistent Sandbox  
**Current Phase:** Tier I  
**Current Party Tier:** Tier I (Levels 1–4)

Beyond Vaelithra is a persistent, asynchronous, West Marches-inspired campaign set in the modern magical city of Kartaloka.

The campaign is NOT a linear adventure path.

Players form different parties for different missions. Missions can produce permanent consequences that affect future missions, factions, NPCs, districts, rumors, and available content.

The world should evolve from player actions rather than forcing every mission into a predetermined central plot.

---

# 2. Canon Priority Rules

When developing future content, use this priority order:

1. **Actual Play Outcome**
2. **Explicit DM Canon Decision**
3. **World State / Canon Lock**
4. **Mission Report**
5. **Session Script**
6. **Mission Design**
7. **Original Planned Outcome**

If an actual-play event differs from the original mission script, the actual event becomes canon unless the DM explicitly retcons it.

Do NOT overwrite player actions to restore the original intended story.

---

# 3. Campaign Design Principles

## 3.1 Persistent World

Mission outcomes permanently affect Kartaloka.

A failed mission may remain failed.

An NPC can die, disappear, escape, become an ally, become an enemy, or become recurring.

A faction may gain or lose influence.

A district may become safer or more dangerous.

New missions may emerge from previous missions.

---

## 3.2 Not Every Mission Is Secretly Important

Some missions are intentionally mundane.

A mission may:

- resolve cleanly;
- have no hidden conspiracy;
- never return;
- create only a small NPC relationship;
- provide a simple reward;
- exist purely to make Kartaloka feel like a living city.

Do NOT retroactively make every mundane mission part of a grand conspiracy.

---

## 3.3 Emergent Storytelling

Major arcs should emerge from accumulated player actions.

Do not force:

> "This was secretly part of the main plot."

Prefer:

> "Because the players did X, this created an opportunity for Y."

# 6. Canon Events

## Event 001 — The Silent Tide / Mission #679

**Mission:** The Silent Tide  
**Mission ID:** #679  
**District:** Wavedock  
**Party Tier:** I  
**Party:**
- Jeff Le Beau
- Kvaraktiss / Kvarat
- John Constantine

### Canon Outcome

Ny. Herawati requested help locating her husband, Slamet, who had been missing for six days.

The party investigated and discovered a clue at Herawati's home leading toward Warehouse No. 12 in Wavedock.

Inside the warehouse, Slamet was being held by the **Silent Tide**, a cult led locally by **Bu Ratih**.

Slamet was being prepared as a sacrifice to an entity known as **The Collector**.

The party discovered that Slamet was not the only prisoner. Two additional captives were also rescued.

A confrontation occurred.

The Silent Tide members at the location were defeated.

Bu Ratih survived and was captured.

Ratih was handed over to **Roy Ivanov** at Westa Tower / The Switchboard.

Recovered items and evidence were subsequently handed to the **Script Order**, reportedly to a person named Roy/Martin/Ivanov in the party report.

### Confirmed Lore

**The Collector**
- Entity worshipped by Silent Tide.
- Appears capable of granting requests in exchange for life sacrifices.
- Exact nature, identity, origin, and power remain unknown.
- Do NOT define The Collector's true nature yet unless explicitly established by the DM.

### Faction Connection

Silent Tide was rumored to have protection or backing from elements associated with **The Wave Family**.

This does NOT currently establish that the entire Wave Family knowingly supports Silent Tide.

Treat this as:

> "Possible corruption / protection by specific individuals"

rather than:

> "The Wave Family is secretly a cult faction."

### Current Status

- Slamet: **Rescued**
- Two additional captives: **Rescued**
- Bu Ratih: **Captured**
- Local Silent Tide cell: **Defeated**
- Silent Tide as a larger organization: **Unknown**
- The Collector: **Active / Unknown**
- Evidence: **Transferred to Script Order**
- Wave Family involvement: **Suspected**

## Event 002 — Every Tuesday Night / Mission #358

**Mission:** Every Tuesday Night  
**Mission ID:** #358  
**District:** Lantern District  
**Party Tier:** I  
**Party:**
- Iggy
- Damian Cole
- Kevin Ivanov

### Canon Outcome

Bu Yanti's occult venue, **The Velvet Séance**, had experienced supernatural disturbances every Tuesday night.

The party investigated the venue and traced the disturbance to the stage.

They discovered a magical circle embedded into the stage.

The circle was not merely decorative.

It was functioning as a **seal containing a Spectre**.

The circle had deteriorated because of the stage decorations.

The party attempted to repair the magical seal but failed.

The Spectre escaped.

The party fought the Spectre and defeated it.

Afterward, the party dismantled the stage to prevent the same situation from happening again.

### Current Status

- Bu Yanti: **Safe**
- The Velvet Séance: **Stage dismantled**
- Spectre: **Defeated**
- Magical seal: **Destroyed / no longer functioning**
- Original occult stage: **No longer intact**
- Future supernatural consequences: **Unknown**

### Existing Rumor

The Spectre reportedly yelled:

before disappearing / being destroyed.

The meaning of **"Xy'lo-" is unknown.**

Do NOT assume it is:
- a person's name;
- a god;

# 10. Mission Outcome Philosophy

When designing follow-up missions, use these outcome categories:

### Direct Consequence

A mission explicitly caused this event.

Example:

> Ratih was captured → someone may want to interrogate, rescue, silence, or recruit her.

### Indirect Consequence

A mission changed circumstances without immediately creating a quest.

Example:

> Silent Tide's Warehouse No. 12 cell was destroyed → someone else may notice the disappearance of their local operation.

### Rumor

Characters hear information without knowing whether it is true.

### Opportunity

The world creates a possible mission, but nobody is required to take it.

### Dormant Thread

An unresolved detail that may never become relevant.

This distinction is important.

Not every unresolved detail needs to become a quest.

# 11. AI Story Development Rules

When helping develop Beyond Vaelithra:

## Always

- Check this memory before proposing consequences.
- Preserve actual player actions.
- Maintain continuity with existing factions and districts.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from rumors.
- Distinguish rumors from DM secrets.
- Allow threads to remain dormant.
- Create consequences that can be discovered organically.
- Preserve the mundane side of Kartaloka.
- Keep magic integrated into modern society.

## Never

- Rewrite a player's actual action because it differs from the script.
- Reveal hidden information to players just because it exists in the DM notes.
- Make every mission secretly connected.
- Turn every NPC into a major character.
- Make every faction part of the same conspiracy.
- Decide the true identity of unresolved entities prematurely.
- Treat rumors as confirmed facts.
- Assume an unresolved mystery must eventually become a major arc.

# 13. Canon Status Legend

Use these labels when expanding the campaign:

**CANON**
> Confirmed by actual play or explicit DM decision.

**KNOWN**
> Information available to player characters through established events.

**RUMOR**
> Characters may have heard it, but it may be incomplete or false.

**SECRET**
> True or potentially true information known only to DM.

**OPEN**
> Deliberately unresolved.

**DORMANT**
> Existing thread that does not currently require development.

**RETIRED**
> Thread or element that is no longer active.
